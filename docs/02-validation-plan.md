# Validation plan: the next 8 weeks

The market research in [01-market-research.md](01-market-research.md) is desk research. It tells you what is plausible. It cannot tell you whether Swedes will pay. Only these five tests can, and all of them cost under a few thousand kronor.

**Run them in parallel with the test shoots, not after.**

---

## Test 1: The aesthetic A/B (free, highest information)

**Question:** are we selling to Swedes or to the diaspora?

**Method.** On both shoot days, shoot the East Asian fantasy look and the Nordic folklore look with the same model, location and hour. Post them as separate pieces of content across Instagram, TikTok and Xiaohongshu. Same caption structure, same time of day, a week apart so they don't cannibalise.

**Measure.** Saves and shares (not likes). Then open the accounts that engaged and classify them: Swedish-born, diaspora, international, other. That classification is tedious and it is the whole point.

**Read it as:**
- Nordic look wins with Swedish accounts → you have a Swedish business. Build that.
- East Asian look wins and engagement is overwhelmingly diaspora → you have a diaspora business. That is a real business, just a smaller and more targeted one. Stop pretending otherwise and market accordingly.
- Neither gets traction → the problem is output quality, not aesthetic direction. Go back to the camera.

---

## Test 2: Price-visible landing page (~200 kr)

**Question:** does anyone want this at 6,900 kr?

A one-page site. Ten images. The package ladder **with prices shown**. One "Enquire about a session" form.

Do not hide the price. A waitlist that doesn't show a price measures curiosity; one that does measures intent, and intent is the only thing worth measuring.

**Threshold:** 25+ genuine enquiries in 8 weeks, of which at least 8 ask about a specific date.

---

## Test 3: Founding client offer (this is the real test)

**Question:** will people give us money?

Sell **6–8 founding-client slots at 1,900 kr**: clearly framed as a discounted first cohort, in exchange for full usage rights to the images and a recorded 20-minute conversation afterwards.

Money changing hands is the only validation that counts. A hundred people saying "that looks amazing" is worth less than one person paying 1,900 kr.

**Threshold:** 6 slots sold within 4 weeks of putting them up. **At least 2 buyers Swedish-born.**

---

## Test 4: Twenty conversations

**Question:** what is the actual job people would hire this for?

Twenty people, 20 minutes each. Mix: Swedish women 25–40, diaspora, a cosplayer, someone who recently had a maternity shoot, someone planning a möhippa. Two of you, split the list.

**Ask about the past, not the future.** "Would you buy this?" produces polite lies. These don't:

1. When did you last pay a photographer for pictures of yourself? What was the occasion?
2. What did it cost? Was it worth it?
3. What did you do with the photos afterwards?
4. Have you ever dressed up as an adult: party, LARP, Halloween, festival? Tell me about it.
5. *(Show the mood board.)* What's your honest first reaction?
6. Who do you know who'd love this? Who'd find it embarrassing?
7. What would have to be true for you to spend 6,900 kr on a day like this?

Question 5 is where you find out whether Jantelagen kills you. Watch faces, not words. A hesitation before a polite answer is data.

**Threshold:** at least 6 of 20 respond to the mood board with unprompted enthusiasm and name a specific occasion they'd use it for.

---

## Test 5: Rental demand probe (near-free)

**Question:** is the rental line real?

Once you own wings and a costume or two, list them for rent in Swedish cosplay, LARP and Medeltidsveckan Facebook groups, and on Blocket. Ask a real price.

**Threshold:** 3 genuine rental enquiries in 6 weeks. If they come in, that is a business with no labour per transaction and it deserves more of your attention than it's currently getting.

---

## Decision gate: mid-November

Sit down together and be honest.

| Result | Decision |
|---|---|
| ≥6 founding clients, ≥2 Swedish, 25+ enquiries, output quality you're proud of | **Go.** Build the real site, register the company, plan the winter studio product |
| Bookings come, but all diaspora | **Pivot narrow.** A deliberate diaspora-focused studio is a genuine business. Market in Chinese and Vietnamese, price for that segment, stop spending energy on Swedish audiences |
| Enquiries but nobody pays | **Price or product problem.** Test a lower tier, or find out what they actually wanted and weren't offered |
| Neither enquiries nor bookings | **Stop.** You will have spent ~10,000 kr and one autumn to learn something true. That is a cheap answer to an expensive question |

Agree now, in writing, while it costs nothing to be honest. The hardest part of this plan is not running the tests: it is believing the results when they're disappointing.
