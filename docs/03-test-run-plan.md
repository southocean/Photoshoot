# Test run plan — Shoot 1 (late Sept) & Shoot 2 (mid Oct), Stockholm

## 0. What this test run is for

It is not to get pretty pictures. It is to answer five questions, cheaply, before anyone spends real money:

1. **Can we produce output that visibly justifies ~6,900 kr?** Look at the gallery afterwards and answer honestly.
2. **How long does it actually take?** Set-up, MUA, per-look changeover, per-usable-frame. You cannot price a service whose duration you don't know.
3. **Which aesthetic gets traction — East Asian fantasy or Nordic folklore?** Same model, same location, same hour, both looks. Post both. Watch the numbers.
4. **Does the logistics chain hold?** Wardrobe, transport, weather, cold, changing, mud, batteries, a model who is happy at hour four.
5. **What breaks?** Write it down as it breaks. This list is the most valuable output of the whole exercise.

**Rule for both days: rent or borrow the wardrobe. Buy nothing you can hire.**

## 3. Locations

Shortlist to scout **at least a week before**, at the same hour of day as the shoot. Never scout at noon for a golden-hour shoot.

| Location | Why | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| **Tyresta nationalpark** (~20 km, ~40 min) | Ancient forest, 400-year-old pines, deep moss and lichen. The best "Nordic jungle" near Stockholm. | It's a **national park** — commercial activity needs permission. For a test run with an unpaid model you're fine; for paid client work, sort the permit. Long carry from parking. |
| **Nackareservatet / Hellasgården** | Mossy rock, water, close to town, easy parking | Popular — busy on a Saturday. Go early or go deep. |
| **Judarskogen (Bromma)** | Small, atmospheric, ponds, very accessible by tunnelbana | Small enough that dog walkers will be in every third frame |
| **Rosendals trädgård / Djurgården** | Garden setting for the Rosenrus concept, rosehips in autumn | Public and commercial premises — **ask permission**, don't just turn up with wings |
| **Långholmen / Skinnarviksberget** | Granite, water, city-edge | Wrong for fairy; right for a moodier direction |

**Choose one primary and one bad-weather alternative within 20 minutes of it.** Do not plan to drive across the county mid-shoot.

**Permission reality check:** allemansrätten does not cover commercial activity. For these two test days with a friend as model and no client paying, you are effectively private individuals and this is a non-issue. The moment money changes hands for a shoot in a reserve, it becomes one. Flagged properly in [01-market-research.md §9](01-market-research.md).

## 4. The four looks

Shoot both aesthetic directions on both days. That comparison is the point.

### Look A — Skogsälva (the reference look)
*Board refs: `backlit-bouquet-fairy`, `skogsalva-white-lace`, `conservatory-green-wings`*

- **Wardrobe:** cream/oat lace slip or tulle gown, bare shoulders, floor-length
- **Hair:** loose waves or a soft updo with escaped strands. Wig optional — shoot with and without
- **Makeup:** dewy, warm, soft flush, glossy lip. Skin left with texture
- **Props:** large translucent butterfly wings, fresh-greenery flower crown, bouquet of white stock or gypsophila, optional pointed ears
- **Light:** sun directly behind, low. Wings glowing. 110 cm silver/white reflector below-front to lift the face. Shoot wide open, ND filter if needed
- **Frames:** full-length backlit hero · three-quarter with bouquet · hands and wings detail · over-shoulder looking back · seated in ground cover

### Look B — Nordic folklore (the adaptation to test)
*No board ref — this is the one we're inventing. Reference John Bauer's forest paintings.*

- **Wardrobe:** undyed linen, wool shawl, heavier and more rustic. Barefoot or simple boots. Less "gown," more "something someone actually wore"
- **Hair:** braided, or long and genuinely unstyled
- **Makeup:** almost none. Bare skin, cold cheeks, no lash extensions
- **Props:** lichen, birch bark, moss, real ferns, a hand-forged-looking brooch. **No tiara. No plastic.**
- **Light:** flat overcast is fine and arguably better. Cool white balance. Let the greens go grey-green
- **Frames:** small figure in a large forest (the Bauer composition — the person is *not* the biggest thing in frame) · half-hidden behind a trunk · close portrait, eyes to camera, no smile

### Look C — Skogsrå (Shoot 2)
*Board ref: `moss-nymph-ferns`*

- Moss and leaf applied to face, shoulders, collarbone
- Camera low, inside the ferns, foreground fronds framing
- Cool, desaturated, low key. Underexpose and hold the shadows
- One mist frame if you can safely run a fog machine or catch real morning mist

### Look D — Autumn Rosenrus (Shoot 2)
*Board ref: `rose-garden-haze`, `interior-romantic-grid`*

- **The seasonality problem:** roses are done by mid-October. Substitute **rosehips, rowan berries, dried hydrangea, oak and maple leaves** — which are more Swedish anyway
- Diffusion filter (Black Pro-Mist 1/4 or 1/2) or a cheap UV filter smeared at the edges with lip balm — that's the haze in the reference
- Drag a chair or a mirror outdoors. Furniture in nature is the whole trick
- Backlight flaring straight into the filter

## 5. Shot list discipline

Per look, you want **6 frames that hang together**, not 200 files. The reference grid on the board is a delivery format as much as a mood: clients buy a *set*.

Minimum per look:
- [ ] 1 full-length hero, backlit
- [ ] 1 three-quarter, eye contact
- [ ] 1 close portrait
- [ ] 1 detail (hands, wings, flowers, hem)
- [ ] 1 environmental — subject small in a big landscape
- [ ] 1 movement — walking, turning, fabric thrown

Shoot the hero frame **first** in each light window. Everything else is a bonus.

## 6. Measurement — the part that makes it a test, not a hobby

Record these on the day and in the two weeks after:

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Set-up → first usable frame | < 30 min |
| Hair + makeup, per look | < 90 min (first) / < 45 min (change) |
| Usable frames per hour of shooting | ≥ 12 |
| Retouching time per delivered image | Track it. If it's over 25 min you cannot deliver 20 images profitably |
| Total person-hours across all three of you, per shoot | Track it. This is your real cost |

Then, posting Look A vs Look B as a paired A/B:

| Metric | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Saves + shares (not likes) | Actual intent |
| Split of engagement: Swedish vs diaspora accounts | **The core question of the whole business** |
| DMs asking "how much / can I book" | The only metric that becomes money |

## 7. Contingency

| If | Then |
|---|---|
| Rain | Go to the greenhouse/orangery fallback, or the studio-glam look (headpiece + black background) which is weather-proof |
| Model cancels | Have a second model on standby. Ask now, not on the Friday |
| Location busy | Have the 20-minute-away alternative pinned in the phone, offline |
| Golden hour clouds over | Blue hour + LED. Or accept it and shoot the overcast look properly — Look B is *better* flat |
| Running late | Cut Look B's frame count, never the golden-hour block |
