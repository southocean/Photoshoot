# Outdoor shoot checklist: Stockholm, autumn

Print this. Tick it twice: once while packing, once in the car before you leave.
The items marked **★** are the ones that ruin a shoot when forgotten.

---

## Camera

- [ ] ★ Camera body (+ second body if you have one)
- [ ] ★ Fast prime: 85mm f/1.4 or f/1.8 is the workhorse for this look
- [ ] 35mm or 50mm for environmental frames
- [ ] Long lens (135mm / 70–200mm): compresses forest background into a wash, very flattering
- [ ] ★ **Batteries: at least 4.** Cold October air halves battery life
- [ ] ★ **Memory cards: more than you think, formatted, plus spares in a separate pocket**
- [ ] Card reader + laptop for a mid-day backup dump
- [ ] Lens cloths ×3 (they get wet and useless immediately)
- [ ] Rain cover for the camera, or a plastic bag and gaffer tape

### Filters: this is where the reference look actually comes from
- [ ] ★ **ND filter (3–6 stop)**: lets you shoot f/1.4 in daylight for that melted background
- [ ] ★ **Diffusion filter**: Black Pro-Mist 1/4 or 1/2. This is the haze in the rose-garden reference
- [ ] CPL to kill leaf glare and deepen greens
- [ ] Cheap UV filter you're willing to smear with vaseline/lip balm for a free dreamy edge

---

## Light

- [ ] ★ **5-in-1 reflector, 110 cm**: silver side for punch, white for gentle fill. The single most important non-camera item
- [ ] ★ **A person to hold it.** A reflector on a stand in wind is a sail
- [ ] Reflector clamp + light stand + **sandbag or water bottle to weigh it down**
- [ ] Scrim / diffusion panel for harsh midday sun
- [ ] Speedlight or small strobe with HSS (Godox AD200-class), for fill against a bright sky
- [ ] Portable LED panel, battery-powered, bi-colour: essential for blue hour
- [ ] Trigger + spare trigger batteries
- [ ] Foldable V-flat / black cloth for negative fill on the shadow side

---

## Atmosphere & effects

- [ ] Fog/haze machine (battery) or hand-held smoke: **check fire rules for the site before using anything that smokes**
- [ ] Spray bottle for dew on leaves and skin
- [ ] Bubble machine or glitter/gold dust for backlit sparkle
- [ ] Small mirror or prism to throw flares into the lens
- [ ] Fan (battery) for hair and fabric movement, or an assistant with a piece of cardboard

---

## Wardrobe & styling on set

- [ ] ★ Costumes, on hangers, in garment bags: **not folded in a bin bag**
- [ ] ★ Wings, packed flat and protected, with the attachment harness tested
- [ ] Steamer (battery/car) or a spray bottle: creases show brutally in backlight
- [ ] Safety pins, bulldog clips, double-sided tape, gaffer tape, sewing kit
- [ ] Extra-long dressmaker's pins for adjusting fit on the spot
- [ ] Nude seamless underwear + strapless bra options
- [ ] ★ Sensible footwear for walking in, plus the shoot shoes (or bare feet)
- [ ] Spare tights, in case of ladders
- [ ] Lint roller

## Hair & makeup kit

- [ ] MUA's own kit: confirm they're bringing it, don't assume
- [ ] ★ Setting spray and blotting papers (wind + cold = shine and running)
- [ ] Touch-up kit for the photographer to carry: powder, lip balm, hair pins, small brush
- [ ] Hairspray, bobby pins, hair ties
- [ ] Mirror: a real one, hand-sized
- [ ] Body glitter / highlighter for backlit skin
- [ ] Face paint or cream colours for the moss/skogsrå look
- [ ] Cotton pads + micellar water + wipes for the changeover
- [ ] Spirit gum + remover for pointed ears

---

## Model comfort: the thing amateurs skip and pros never do

A cold, uncomfortable model photographs as a cold, uncomfortable model. In Stockholm in October this is not optional.

- [ ] ★ **Thermos of hot drink**, two if there are two models
- [ ] ★ **Big blanket or duvet** for between takes
- [ ] ★ **Pop-up changing tent** (or a big poncho, or a car with tinted windows)
- [ ] Thermal base layers to wear under the costume where they won't show
- [ ] Hand warmers: the disposable stick-on kind
- [ ] Dry towel, spare socks
- [ ] Food. Real food, not just snacks. Nobody smiles at hour five on an empty stomach
- [ ] Insect repellent (September) and antihistamine
- [ ] Rubber boots for walking to the spot in, changed out of just before the frame
- [ ] Somewhere warm to retreat to: a car with the heater on counts

---

## Grip, safety, site

- [ ] Ground sheet / picnic blanket to keep costumes off wet moss
- [ ] Kneeling pad for low-angle shots
- [ ] Small folding step stool for high angles
- [ ] Bin bags, for rubbish, for wet kit, as emergency rain covers
- [ ] ★ First aid kit. Mossy granite is slippery
- [ ] Head torch ×2: it gets dark fast and you will be packing in the dark in October
- [ ] Power bank for phones
- [ ] Offline map of the location downloaded on someone's phone
- [ ] Trolley or wheelbarrow if the carry from parking is long (Tyresta: it is)
- [ ] Parking payment sorted before you're out of signal

---

## Admin

- [ ] ★ **Signed model release**, including explicit marketing/social-media permission
- [ ] Call sheet: times, addresses, phone numbers, what to wear/bring: sent the day before
- [ ] Cash for parking / small fees
- [ ] Permission confirmed in writing if it's private or commercial land
- [ ] Insurance details on your phone
- [ ] Weather checked at T-12h; decision made and communicated

---

## After the shoot, same day

- [ ] ★ **Back up the cards. Two copies, two locations.** Before anyone sleeps
- [ ] Note the metrics from [03-test-run-plan.md §6](03-test-run-plan.md) while they're fresh
- [ ] Write the "what broke" list immediately: you will forget by Monday
- [ ] Costumes hung to dry, not stuffed in a bag; damage logged for the rental return
- [ ] Batteries on charge, cards formatted only after backup is verified
- [ ] Send the model a thank-you and three quick previews within 24 hours
