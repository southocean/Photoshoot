# Props & wardrobe — test run

**Governing rule: rent or borrow everything you can. Buy only what you cannot hire and will definitely reuse.**
The purpose of the test run is to find out what you need. Buying a wardrobe before you know that is how this idea quietly turns into 40,000 kr of dresses in a friend's basement.

Prices below are rough orders of magnitude for budgeting, not quotes.

## Buy — the short list

Only these. Everything else is hired or improvised.

### Wings — the hero prop
The single item that makes or breaks every frame on the mood board. Cheap wings look cheap instantly and there is no saving them in post.

- **What to look for:** large (1.2 m+ span), translucent, *visibly veined*, semi-rigid so they hold shape in wind
- **What to avoid:** opaque fabric wings, glitter-plastic Halloween wings, anything under ~400 kr
- **Budget:** 800–2,500 kr for a good pair. Etsy makers, cosplay wing makers, or commission a Swedish cosplayer — that also buys you a relationship with the LARP/cosplay scene
- **Test the harness before the day.** Wings that sag or blow sideways cost you the hero frame
- ★ Order **now** — handmade wings have long lead times

### Fabric — the cheapest way to look expensive
- 6–10 m of cream/oat **tulle** and **silk chiffon**, 400–900 kr
- 3 m of undyed **linen** for the Nordic look, ~500 kr
- A large **wool shawl** or blanket, secondhand
- Fabric solves posing problems, movement problems and modesty problems at once. Buy more than you think.

### Headpieces & hair
- Florist wire, floral tape, secateurs — build flower crowns from **fresh greenery** the morning of the shoot (eucalyptus, ivy, olive, ferns). Fresh reads real; plastic reads Halloween. ~150 kr of supplies
- One good **wig** if you're testing the copper-hair look: 600–1,500 kr for something that isn't obviously synthetic. Optional — my recommendation is to shoot real hair first
- Prosthetic **elf ears** + spirit gum: ~250 kr

### Small props
- Silk/paper butterflies on wire — 100–200 kr
- Fresh flowers, per shoot day: 300–600 kr (Blomsterlandet, or the Söderhallarna/market florists early morning)
- Autumn substitutes for Shoot 2: rosehips, rowan berries, oak and maple branches, dried hydrangea — **free, foraged, and more Swedish than roses**
- A small ornate mirror and a chair — secondhand/Blocket, 300–800 kr. Furniture outdoors is the whole trick in the rose-garden reference
- Moss, lichen, birch bark — foraged. Take from the ground, not from living trees

### Consumables
- Body glitter, cream face colours, spirit gum, setting spray: ~500 kr

## Rough test-run budget

| Line | Low | High |
|---|---|---|
| Wings (buy) | 800 | 2,500 |
| Costume rental, 2 weeks | 1,500 | 4,000 |
| Fabric | 900 | 1,800 |
| Wig + ears (optional) | 0 | 1,750 |
| Flowers & greenery, 2 days | 600 | 1,200 |
| Props, secondhand | 300 | 800 |
| Makeup consumables | 300 | 700 |
| MUA — 2 days | 0 (do it yourselves) | 9,000 |
| Transport, parking, food | 800 | 1,600 |
| **Total** | **≈ 5,200** | **≈ 23,350** |

**Aim for the low column.** If the test run costs 23,000 kr you have learned nothing about whether the business works — you have only learned that you can spend money. Do the makeup yourselves for the test run if any of you can; it also tells you what an MUA actually needs to do, which you need to know before you hire one.

## What to log while you use it

For every item, after each shoot day:

| Field | Why |
|---|---|
| Did it appear in a keeper frame? | Half your props won't. Don't buy those again |
| Time to fit / apply | Feeds the schedule and therefore the price |
| Survived the day? | Rental-line viability |
| Would a client pay extra for it? | The add-on menu writes itself from this column |

This log becomes the buying list for the real wardrobe — grounded in evidence instead of Pinterest.
